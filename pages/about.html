About Muzita Life Legacy

MUZITA LIFE LEGACY (PVO 03/24) is a Private Voluntary Organisation supporting Zimbabwe's elderly with care & community. We provide home visits, health support & social connections for those needing dignity & companionship.

Our Vision

Empowering a compassionate community that values and supports the elderly, promoting dignity, respect and inclusivity for all.

Our Objectives

As registered under the Private Voluntary Organisations Act [Chapter 17:05] — PVO 03/24

I. Psychosocial Support

To provide psychosocial support to older persons in communities.

II. Community Capacities

To strengthen community capacities for the care of older persons.

III. Advocacy & Participation

To advocate for meaningful involvement and participation of older persons in issues that affect them.

IV. Residential Care Services

To provide residential care services for the displaced older persons.

Our Activities

Muzita Life Legacy implements a range of activities designed to support and empower elderly community members and their caregivers. Our programs are built on the principle that elderly care is a shared community responsibility.

Through our established support groups, home-based care services, and capacity building programs, we create sustainable solutions that address the unique challenges faced by Zimbabwe's elderly population. We believe in fostering independence while providing the necessary support for dignity and quality of life.

Our community outreach and engagement activities help build awareness about elderly care issues and mobilize community resources to create a more inclusive and supportive environment for our senior citizens.
